# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in GVP-Guard: NightEye AI a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Being respectful and welcoming to different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community
- Being honest about what the system can and cannot do

**Examples of unacceptable behaviour:**

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory comments or personal attacks
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or sexualised language and imagery
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Project-Specific Standards

Because this project handles video, vehicles and potentially identifiable information, contributors must also:

- **Not** upload or share real CCTV footage, number plates or personal data in issues, pull requests or discussions
- **Not** use the project to target, harass, surveil or falsely accuse any individual
- **Not** misrepresent the system as providing automatic guilt, fines or legally admissible evidence
- Respect that final decisions rest with authorised human officers

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction:** private written warning and clarification of expected behaviour.
2. **Warning:** warning with consequences for continued behaviour; a period of no interaction.
3. **Temporary Ban:** temporary ban from project spaces for serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent Ban:** permanent ban for sustained or severe violations.
